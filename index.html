01 / Applied AILive project

Ancient wisdom.
Grounded answers.

Gita Reflection connects everyday questions with relevant Bhagavad Gita verses. A working exploration of retrieval-augmented generation, with the source passage and translation alongside each reflection.

  • Amazon Bedrock
  • Vector search
  • RAG
Explore Gita Reflection ↗
Behind the build

The questionHow can an AI reflection stay connected to the text it draws from?

What I’m working onA retrieval pipeline across 700 verses, exploring embeddings, query expansion, reranking and source validation.

What I’m learningHow relevance, response time and faithfulness change with retrieval choices. Held-out questions and pipeline timings help me examine those trade-offs.

02 / ObservabilityInteractive demo

Making system behaviour visible.

An operations console that connects service health, infrastructure metrics and incident timelines. Explore simulated failures and recovery through the questions an operator would ask.

  • FastAPI
  • Service health
  • Incident simulation
Explore the operations demo ↗
Behind the build

The questionWhat’s degraded, what changed, and where should I look next?

The approachBring metrics, logs and incident timelines into one view. A separate runbook-retrieval experiment explores how AI could support investigation.

The boundaryTelemetry and incident summaries are illustrative, not live production monitoring. Sample data keeps the dashboard usable without its backend.

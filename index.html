Gita Reflection

gita.atla.in live

Bring a real situation: a difficult decision, a restless mind, or a conversation you cannot resolve. The app finds one supporting verse from the Gita's 700 and shows the Sanskrit, transliteration, translation, and a plain-language reflection.

The answer is grounded by design. Retrieval, fusion, and local reranking select the verse before generation, so the final model works from evidence instead of inventing scripture.

  • Retrieval-augmented generation
  • Vector search
  • Reranking
  • Grounded output
  • Latency budgeting

Observability

obs.atla.in live

A cloud-operations console for watching a simulated system change under pressure: SLOs, latency, error rate, services, logs, and incidents.

Trigger a traffic spike or database error burst, follow the blast radius through the service map, then ask the assistant for a root-cause summary and mitigation plan. The data is synthetic by design; the operating questions are real.

  • SLO design
  • Incident simulation
